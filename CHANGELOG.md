# Changelog

## 2.12.2

- Show all six Easy-mode hearts in compact phone layouts.
- Keep browser and Android presentation assets synchronized.

## 2.12.1

- Make the Down control align Willy with nearby open shafts, including while resting on an edge.
- Make held jumps more forgiving when entering narrow upward passages.
- Preserve collision protection for solid floors, locks, hazards and movement away from an opening.

## 2.12.0

- Add Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty with 6, 4 and 3 starting hearts.
- Adjust hostile movement speed by difficulty while preserving platform speed.
- Keep God Mode independent from difficulty.
- Remove the settings test-sound button.

## 2.11

- Modernize Willy, enemies, moving objects and dangerous surfaces.
- Add one moving bonus heart to every world.
- Make hazards cost health with a recovery window instead of immediately ending a run.
- Improve damage audio, character animation and the Swiss cheese scene.
- Add music volume control and improve mobile level-selection scrolling.

## 2.10

- Introduce the high-resolution cartoon presentation while retaining the original map geometry and themes.
- Restore clear space-station door labels and completion checks.
- Modernize cans, lollipops, keys, locks, EXIT cards, lifts and hazards.

## 2.9

- Add the illustrated adventure home screen.
- Make cans and lollipops optional score items in every world.
- Add separate assisted high scores.

## 2.6–2.8

- Add the first-play guide, recommended starting world, optional checkpoints and shareable completion screen.
- Add a recorded soundtrack for every world theme, victory and finale.
